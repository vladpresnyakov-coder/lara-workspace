Lara Workspace Vlad

A private personal-assistant integration operated for its account owner. Lara helps the owner review Gmail, prepare correspondence and reconcile planned tasks and calendar events with the owner's Mosk planner.

Google account access

  • Gmail: read messages and attachments; prepare drafts. An email is sent only following the owner's explicit instruction for that message.
  • Google Calendar: read calendars and create or edit the owner's events within the owner's instructions.
  • Google Tasks: read and manage tasks within the owner's instructions.
  • Mosk: add missing Google counterparts of scheduled Mosk records under the owner's standing permission. Google-only items and existing-record conflicts are presented for the owner's decision.

This integration is for the account owner's personal use. It is not a public mailbox, a public calendar, a mailing service or an autonomous email sender. This website contains no email, task, financial or calendar records.

Privacy and control

Google authorization is handled through Google's OAuth consent screen. The owner can revoke access in their Google Account settings. The application's technical permissions do not remove its instruction-based restrictions.

Privacy policy

For support or a data-control request, use the developer contact shown on the Google consent screen, or contact the repository owner through this project's issue tracker. Do not post private account data or credentials in a public issue.
