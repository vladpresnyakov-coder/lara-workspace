Lara Workspace Vlad

Privacy policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026.

Purpose and operator

Lara Workspace Vlad is a private integration used by its account owner for personal-assistant functions. It connects the owner's authorized Google account and Mosk planner. It is not offered as a public multi-user service.

Data accessed

Within the permissions granted by the owner, the integration can access Gmail messages, headers, attachments and drafts; Google calendar lists and event details; and Google Tasks lists and tasks. It also processes the owner's Mosk planning records and synchronization identifiers. OAuth access and refresh tokens are used for authorized API access.

How data is used

  • Reviewing and summarizing correspondence and user-specified attachments.
  • Preparing drafts and sending the owner's specifically instructed messages.
  • Reading and reconciling calendars and tasks, adding permitted counterparts of scheduled Mosk records, and reporting differences for the owner's decision.
  • Producing the owner's requested reports, reminders and operational records.

The integration does not use Google account data to sell advertising or build marketing profiles. It does not sell Google user data. Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Processing and transfers

Requested content may be processed by the assistant's configured AI service providers as necessary to perform the owner's requested functions. Requested results may be sent to the owner's configured delivery channels, such as their private Telegram conversation. Only information necessary for the requested function should be used. Email and calendar invitations to other people require the owner's applicable authorization. No private Google data is published on this informational website or in its public source repository.

Storage and safeguards

Operational copies of requested messages, attachments, reports, synchronization mappings and diagnostic records may be stored on the owner's assistant server. Credentials are stored separately with restricted filesystem access and must not be included in public pages, repositories or reports. This statement describes the integration's handling policy; it is not a claim of Google verification, a security certification or a guarantee against all security risks.

Retention, revocation and deletion

The owner can revoke Google access at Google Account connections. Revocation stops authorized new API access but does not by itself erase previously saved reports or files. The owner can instruct the assistant operator to delete retained integration data and credentials. Operational records are kept only as needed for the owner's functions and follow-up instructions; no fixed automatic deletion interval is represented here.

Contact and changes

Use the developer contact displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen or contact the repository owner for questions or a private contact route. Never publish passwords, tokens, messages or personal records in a public issue. Changes to this policy will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
